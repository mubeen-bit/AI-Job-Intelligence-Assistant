Job Description

We are seeking a DevOps Engineer for a permanent/direct hire opportunity in Bangalore, India. In this Agile environment, you will work between the SQA and Dev teams to design and develop high performance solutions for critical applications. The responsibilities of this role are as follows:

Design, deploy and maintain DevOps platforms, systems and infrastructure.
Build, deployment, monitoring, and troubleshooting of cloud-native infrastructure and technology components using Kubernetes, Docker and Big Data technologies.
Work across all development teams to encourage best practices for continuous delivery and configuration management and to identify missing tools and automation.
Develop, implement, and optimize the software build and deployment pipelines.
Development of solutions for the automation of system provisioning and deployment.
Implement monitoring platforms across infrastructure, applications and networks.
Improve and build on kernel performance, database and storage optimizations.
Manage Linux clusters and containers using tools such as Ansible, Chef, Puppet.
Troubleshoot and resolve live production issues by analyzing logs from different sources, follow up with developers, platform engineer and other teams on the resolution.
Perform software installations and upgrades for the DevOps services platform and associated systems during industry-standard maintenance windows
Direct involvement with 24/7/365 production environment.

Qualifications

Bachelor's in Computer Science, Telecommunications or equivalent work experience.
Three years of hands-on experience in DevOps engineering.
Three years of hands-on experience with automation/configuration management tools (Puppet, Salt, Chef, Ansible, etc.)
Three years of hands-on experience in multi-server platforms and Linux operating system
Three years of hands-on experience with container technologies and management platforms (Kubernetes, Docker, Elastic Container Service)
Experience delivering mission-critical services in a SaaS/SaaP or PaaS

Functional Knowledge and Skills Required

Ability to knowledge share and work cross-functionally, in an Agile environment
Ability to translate standards to software requirements
Experience with RedHat OpenShift and Kubernetes
Hands-on development capability; Three years of Kotlin / Java / JVM languages
