# Job Title: Java Developer

## Job ID: 2315

## Description
Process Control Network Security / Developer – Fort Collins, CO*
Trihydro Corporation, a mid-sized engineering and environmental consulting firm with a national footprint, is seeking a dynamic, talented software engineer to join our team. This position will focus on database design and development, as well as software development.

Improving our communities and the environment is at the heart of Trihydro’s mission. We achieve our mission by empowering our employees to develop solutions that exceed our clients’ expectations. We understand our employees are our number one asset; treating them as such has fostered a strong company culture that has spurred our growth and diversification over the past 35 years. We need motivated team members who are interested in supporting our continued pursuit of our mission, growth and diversification.
Position Overview*
As a member of our team, you will be the primary point of contact interfacing between Trihydro project teams that manage remediation system operations or other data-intensive systems, and client network security representatives and/or equipment, instrumentation, and software suppliers and subcontractors. In addition, you will support development, testing, and analysis of existing database solutions as well as play an integral part in designing and implementing database solutions for new products. In addition to database work the ideal applicant also will be well versed in software development and backend service integration. Users should have familiarity with databases such as SQL Server, Oracle, MongoDB, and Cosmos.
Location*
This position is based in Fort Collins, CO, and will require periodic visits to work with teams located in other office locations (including Laramie, Wyoming). Laramie is home to Trihydro’s Corporate Headquarters, while our Fort Collins office is located in a thriving community offering almost limitless activities.
Responsibilities*
In this role you will have responsibilities for Process Control Networks and Process Logic Controls (PCN / PLCs); database design and development; and management with software development as time allows and aligns with your skills. Key roles and associated areas of responsibility include
*PCN / PLC Champion*
o External-facing liaison between client PCN Security Coordinator and Trihydro project teams to help each team make sure they are aware of client-required PCN requirements

o Internal-facing liaison with Trhydro IT staff who can help with IT/server/system requirements

o Over time, move from compliance (reacting to requirements) to proactive management and anticipating data integrity and security issues

o For Trihydro teams designing new remediation systems, or teams contemplating major modifications to existing systems, serve as a sounding board for data management / integrity / security considerations
Data Management Support*
o Support teams in design, maintenance, and optimization of existing and new database solutions

o Support teams with data access / security risk assessment

o Audit, troubleshoot, and maintain database security and authentication

o Follow current and evolving practices and help teams apply them to existing database solutions

o Assess and cultivate long-term strategic goals for database management
Software Development Responsibilities*
o Well versed in object-oriented software languages such as C# or Java

o Design and build data-drive applications

o Focus on Database interaction and data operations (CRUD)

o Knowledgeable of data structures and associated performance

o Well versed in Agile development

o REST service development
