# Job Title: iOS Developer

## Job ID: 1768

## Description
We are looking for an expert developer with superstar iOS development skills. Working as part of a cross-functional team following agile practices to deliver iOS apps. You will be expected to follow best software practices including testing, test automation, code reviews, refactoring and pairing.

Key Responsibilities:

Deliver and maintain new and existing iOS Apps.
Support your team to meet sprint commitments.
Ensure that impediments of any kind are escalated and long term solutions pursued.
Deliver high quality well tested software that meets agreed requirements.
Promote best software development practices.
Collaborate.
Support and promote agile practices including Scrum and XP.

Knowledge, Skills and Experience:

Minimum 3 years experience developing non trivial iOS Apps that have been released in the app store.
Good Knowledge on the Libraries/API's as Facebook iOS SDK, Twitter iOS SDK, Google Analytics API, Flurry, Amazon API, Google Maps API, Google Ads API, Facebook API, Facebook SDK, Facebook Open Graph API and OpenGL.
Strong proficiency in Swift and associated Design Patterns.
Solid understanding of software engineering.
Experience writing tests, automating these and adopting TDD desirable.
Good knowledge of web technologies and paradigms (JSON, Ajax, AMF, HTTP, REST).
Team working experience.
Nice to have a knowledge and understanding of concurrent programming.
Nice to have an understanding of profiling tools and memory management.
