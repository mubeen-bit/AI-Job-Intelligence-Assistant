## Description
Why Ericsson?

Ericsson is driving 5G standardization and was the largest contributor to the standardization body in 3GPP during 2018. Pioneering customers select us as their 5G partner and we are first with commercial live networks in four continents. BU: BMAS


Location: Noida

Our Exciting Opportunity!

We are hiring IP Network SME to be a part of our team.

Responsibilities!


Deploy & drive end-to-end technical solutions in IP & Packet Transport Domains across – IPRAN/Mobility, CEN Networks, IP-MPLS, MPLS-TP, Packet Backbone etc.

Develop deployment technical Transport solution presentations and implementation procedures and perform C-Level customer presentations on services solutions.

Strong team player capable to support Integration teams in troubleshooting during deployment & Migration Phases of Project on product, performance & solution level.

Ensure 1st time Right Methods and prepare Root Cause Analysis for outages.

Deliver Project Milestones as per agreed timelines secure customer acceptance.

Travel and work in Customer Project Locations and work across cross-domain teams.

Excellent IP/MPLS CEN Network competence with ability to independently work in areas of E2E QoS, Packet Transport Network Dimensioning, Optimization, Integration & Service Migrations.

Ability to learn & work in cutting edge areas like SDN & Cloud Infrastructure

To be successful in the role one must have


8 to 15 Years of work experience

CCNA or JNCIA & CCIE (R&S) or JNCIS or JNCIP (Service Provider)

– Strong IPv6 & e2e QoS competence & ability to troubleshoot e2e IP Transport Network

– Design, Configuration & Deployment of CISCO & Juniper Routing & Switching Products for Mobility & Enterprise solutions.

– Proven experience in designing/implementing/managing IP/MPLS CEN networks including swaps & migrations

– Hands-on experience in deploying large (100 Gig ring) Core & Aggregation Routers

– Familiar with design concepts related to IP addressing plan, Routing, Security, Switching, Tunneling, VPNs, layer -2 concepts such as VLANs, COS, Firewall, NTP, AAA, Basic Network level knowledge of 2G,3G&4G interfaces & functionalities
