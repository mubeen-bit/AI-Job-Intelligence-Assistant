# Job Title: Software Engineer

## Job ID: 824

## Description
Vertafore is a leading technology company whose innovative software solution are advancing the insurance industry. Our suite of products provides solutions to our customers that help them better manage their business, boost their productivity and efficiencies, and lower costs while strengthening relationships.

Our mission is to move InsurTech forward by putting people at the heart of the industry. We are leading the way with product innovation, technology partnerships, and focusing on customer success.

Our fast-paced and collaborative environment inspires us to create, think, and challenge each other in ways that make our solutions and our teams better.

We are headquartered in Denver, Colorado, with offices across the U.S., Canada, and India.

JOB DESCRIPTION

As a Technical Lead, Software Development Engineering in Test, you will be instrumental in Vertafore’s ongoing success within the insurance industry. Our leads work with our development, product teams focusing on the providing the best products for our valued customers. This is a fast paced, ever changing environment. We continually challenge ourselves to exceed objectives, remove barriers, meet crucial deadlines, and maintain multiple projects, all while pioneering ways for efficiency with a team player attitude. If you have a passion for product quality, love technology, and enjoy thinking outside the box, you are who we are looking for.

Core Requirements and Responsibilities:

Essential job functions included but are not limited to the following:

· Create testing and automation strategies and approaches in alignment with overall organizational goals

· Write test cases

· Establishes overall test infrastructure, creates and coordinates test systems

· Coordinates testing across products and establishes best practices

· Reviews testing documentation provided by others on the team, providing feedback and integrating documentation

Knowledge, Skills and Abilities:

· As a member of the engineering team who can determine the next level of Test automation.

· Have experience in designing automation test frameworks especially for tiered architecture.

· Experience in creating and driving the Automation Strategy.

· Mentor an automation team.

· Genuine hands on Coding experience.

· Good Experience with Java 8 or above.

· Test automation experience with good Knowledge of Selenium, Cucumber.

· Experience with Version Control, Code Collaboration, and Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment Pipelines.

· Good Knowledge of API testing tools.

· Experience using various bug tracking tools such as Rally, and test management tools such as qTest.

· Experience with Agile software development.

· Experience in Test Automation Tool / Frameworks

· Experience with SQL Server is a plus.

· Experience with JMeter is a plus.

Qualifications:

· Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science (or related technical field) or equivalent practical experience.

· 6+Years of Experience in Testing/SDET role with 3+Years of lead experience.

· Ability to define problems, collect data, analyze data, establish facts, and draw valid conclusions.

· Ability to manage a broad range of deliverables in an ever-changing environment.

· Ability to communicate and work effectively with cross-functional teams.

· Ability to comprehend documentation clearly and effectively.

· Excellent verbal and written communication skills.

· Working knowledge of OOP coding concepts.

· Strong time management and organizational skills.

· Detail oriented; demonstrated ability to handle multiple strategic projects and priorities with efficiency and accuracy.

· An inherent drive to solve problems, collaborate with others and mentor team members.
