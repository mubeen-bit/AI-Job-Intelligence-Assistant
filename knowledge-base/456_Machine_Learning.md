## Description
Why Tax Technology? Amazon has it all: AWS; Large volumes of data; Complex businesses and transactions; and a massive geographic footprint. Tax must deal with these challenges and the complexity of rules across thousands of jurisdictions. Tax technology provides an opportunity to innovate, design and build new platforms and services on AWS.
Amazons seeks an innovative, capable, and motivated individual to be a Software Development Manager (SDM) on the Direct Tax Technology team, based in Hyderabad, India. The role is responsible for ensuring the tax professionals of Amazons Global Tax Reporting and Compliance organization (R&C) are supported in their mission with world-class technology and processes built on AWS. The impact of this role is evidenced by the ability to innovate robust and scalable capabilities that empower our customers and the organization to continue to meet the unique challenges of Amazon.
As a Software Development Manager, you will lead and build a team of world-class software engineers. You and your team will drive product/platform development and partner with other Amazon teams and businesses to deliver AWS based systems and services. Amazon SDMs possess the ability to deliver results, think big, make sound decisions and build highly-scalable/performant systems for the long term. This role is expected to lead by example to earn the trust of your team and customers.
