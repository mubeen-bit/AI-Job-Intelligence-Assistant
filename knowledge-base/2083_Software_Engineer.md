# Job Title: Software Engineer

## Job ID: 2083

## Description
The ideal candidate for this position would be a passionate Web Development technical lead proficient in building and running modern, scalable rich web applications at scale. At BlueJeans we are changing the way the world collaborates on video, and the candidate will be an integral part of the team that helps scale our service to meet the spiralling demand induced by work-from-home necessity.

The ideal candidate will...
Have a passion for technology with strong design and problem solving skills
Have excellent oral and written communication skills
Have experience designing, implementing and deploying large scalable web services
Be proficient in ES6/HTML5/css3 and Javascript based Front-End’s such as React (or Backbone/Angular/Ember)
Be proficient in at-least one server side language such as Node.js.
Have a good understanding of event-driven architectures.
Have experience working with RESTful APIs and common RESTful design patterns.
Be able to communicate and work well within a geographically distributed team


Requirements:
8+ years of product development experience
2+ years of experience technically leading a high performing product development team
Experience of working with RESTful APIs
Excellent knowledge of OOPS & Design patterns.
BE/B.Tech in Computer Science or related field
Familiarity with deployments on Amazon or Azure
Familiarity with setup of Monitoring/Alert/Analytics systems to track health of services in real time
Interest in mentoring junior developers on the team in their technical growth
Habit of maintaining a tight feedback loop for developers and internal users by doing test-driven development at the unit- and acceptance-test level for fast feedback


You get bonus points for...
Real time communication via webrtc.
Browser extensions for chrome or firefox.
Understanding latest developments in key technologies and how this impacts your work.
Understanding of database technologies (SQL and NoSQL such as couchbase and mongodb) as well as API cache design and caching technologies.
Experience in scaling large high transaction volume web services
Experience in mobile development
Contributions to open source projects
Experience in influencing partners like Microsoft, Google, and others in the areas of WebRTC and web standards, managed runtime (JavaScript, Typescript etc), React, and open source efforts


Verizon recently acquired BlueJeans and plans to integrate BlueJeans employees into Verizon, including its compensation and benefits programs, in due course. This position will be part of that planned integration.
