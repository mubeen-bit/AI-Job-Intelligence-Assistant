Deep understanding of collaboration protocols such as, TCP, UDP, SIP, SCCP, QoS, H.323, G.711, H.264 and SVC

Expert knowledge of Firewalls, Access Lists, NAT, VPNs and Network Security

Experience with IPSec Site-to-Site VPNs, MPLS, Fiber Optic, and Gigabit and Metro Ethernet

Expert knowledge of Network Systems including Routers, Switches, Firewalls and Servers

Ability to plan IP utilization and networks, DHCP, DNS, redundancy, failover, and high availability

In depth experience with Cisco wireless solutions

Ability to identify, troubleshoot, and resolve network, application and performance issues

Network security experience.

LAN and WAN experience.

Strong understanding of Data centre infrastructure.

Ability to think through problems and visualize solutions.

Ability to create accurate network diagrams and documentation for design and planning network communication systems.

Provides specific detailed information for hardware and software selection.
