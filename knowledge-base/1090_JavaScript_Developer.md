# Job Title: JavaScript Developer

## Description
Job Description:

Atom CTO are looking for a full stack JavaScript developer to join their international team. You should be experienced in developing web applications and APIs in JavaScript using frameworks like Express, Fastify, Feathers JS etc. and be comfortable with creating client applications in Vue/Nuxt/React/Next that integrate with third-party APIs. This role will be a mix of R&D work to discover what are the latest and greatest trends as well as building solutions to spec.We believe that work should be balanced with personal time, so you won't find long hours or be asked to sacrifice your free time for projects. We all agree on our work for the week and just get on with it!

Willingness to learn new platforms and adapt as requirements change.
Coordinating with other teams and team members to integrate systems.
Managing your own time and reporting your progress.

Unit testing / test driven development
Result oriented attitude
