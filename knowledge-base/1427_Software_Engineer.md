# Job Title: Software Engineer

## Job ID: 1427

## Description
Vista Equity Partners is the world's leading private equity firm for Enterprise Software and recently invested $1.5B in the Reliance Jio Digital Platform. As part of the Vista portfolio, Integral Ad Science (IAS) is a NYC-headquartered global technology and data company that builds verification, optimization, and analytics solutions for the advertising industry. We are looking for a UI Engineer to join our new and fast-growing Engineering team in Pune. If you are excited by technology that has the power to handle hundreds of thousands of transactions per second; collect tens of billions of events each day; and evaluate thousands of data-points in real-time all while responding in just a few milliseconds, then IAS is the place for you!

The most valuable services we provide are reporting and analytics. Our data provides valuable insights into ad campaign performance, helping Advertisers drive advertising traffic and increasing ad conversions. As a UI Engineer you will build and expand upon the IAS reporting framework and UI for IAS' analytics and reporting software products. You will integrate reports and dashboards into the IAS UI platform and implement critical features in our custom report builder tool using state of the art technologies and tools.

The ideal candidate is detailed-oriented with a keen eye for great user interfaces and an excellent understanding of user workflows and user experience.

What you'll get to do:
Innovate and develop for our core UI Platform
Integrate 3rd party reports into our UI Platform
Design and develop UI components and features specific to custom reporting
Participate in agile meetings and daily stand ups
Work closely with Product teams and other cross-functional teams
You should apply if you have most of this experience:
3-5+ years of recent experience with UI development
AngularJS, React, or other UI frameworks
Fluency with HTML5 and CSS
NodeJS, gulp, and Bower
Testing implementation using Karma and Jasmine
AWS services such as EC2, S3, Lamba, and SQS
Full software development life cycle, agile development and continuous integration
Exposure to Business Intelligence tools such as Looker, Tableau, and/or JasperSoft
About Integral Ad Science

Integral Ad Science (IAS) is the global market leader in digital ad verification, offering technologies that drive high-quality advertising media. IAS equips advertisers and publishers with both the insight and technology to protect their advertising investments from fraud and unsafe environments as well as to capture consumer attention, and drive business outcomes. Founded in 2009, IAS is headquartered in New York with global operations in 18 offices across 13 countries. IAS is part of the Vista Equity Partners portfolio of software companies. For more on how IAS is powering great impressions for top publishers and advertisers around the world, visit integralads.com.
