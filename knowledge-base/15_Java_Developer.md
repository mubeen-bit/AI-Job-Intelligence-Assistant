# Job Title: Java Developer

## Job ID: 15

## Description
Overview:

Western National is seeking a highly collaborative and influential front-end developer to join our team in developing exceptional experiences. This exceptional opportunity offers someone a chance to contribute to a highly impactful organizational initiative that will transform how our customers interact with our company.

The ideal candidate has 5+ years of Software Development experience with strong experience in modern Javascript (ES6+) and Node. The candidate would ideally have experience with Vue or similar web frameworks, and there is a high preference for Java experience. Come join our team for opportunities to grow your skills and be a part of something truly transformative.

Responsibilities:
Work in an agile environment with other IT teams.
Collaborate with business partners to implement new features.
Mentor Jr level team members.
Estimate effort and assist in prioritization of business feature requests.
Identify and resolve software application issues.
Prepare technical design specifications, flow charts, and data flow diagrams.
Develop and execute unit tests.
Implement software solutions based on business requirements.
Identify and implement process enhancements and automation.
Regular interaction with business partners across organization.
Occasional interaction with external vendors.
Qualifications:
5+ years of software development experience.
Proficient in modern Javascript (ES6+) and Node.
Strong experience with HTML5 / CSS3 / SCSS.
Moderate to strong experience with Vue or similar web frameworks (React, Angular).
Moderate to strong experience developing or consuming REST web services.
Moderate to strong experience developing with Java is helpful.
Experience with Javascript testing frameworks (Jest, Mocha, etc.).
Experience with build tools such as npm and webpack.
Experience with version control (SVN or Git).
Experience with Dev Ops and/or continuous integration (CI) tools (Jenkins) is useful.
Experience in an agile environment.
Strong verbal and written communication skills.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
B.S. Degree in Computer Science or equivalent experience.
