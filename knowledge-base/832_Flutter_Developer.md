# Job Title: Flutter Developer

## Description
We are looking for a Mobile Application developer fluent in the Flutter framework and passionate about building usable mobile applications. Our development center is located in Chennai, India.

Ability to understand the business requirement, scope of work, and use-cases of the project.
Minimum 1 year of working knowledge in the Flutter framework.
Minimum 3 years of experience in mobile application development.
Detailed understanding of the iOS and Android ecosystem.
Practical knowledge in the Mobile Application development lifecycle including deployment.
Basic knowledge in writing specification, functional, and technical implementation documents.
Practical knowledge in Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) and understanding of SOLID principles.
Ability to manage codebase using the version control system (Git).
Basic experience in working with RESTful/GraphQL based APIs as backend.
Basic experience in using Firebase for data storage and notifications.
General requirements:
The attitude of being open and curious. Self-motivated to keep learning about technology.
A heart to be a team player and willing to work and compete (friendly competition!) with like-minded people in the team.
A challenge-seeking mind with a will to think and find solutions to complex code problems.
Eagerness to design solutions that performs and scales really well.
Paranoia about cybersecurity and obsession for building secure software.
A burning desire to write beautiful, efficient, maintainable, near-perfect, and future-you friendly code.
If you feel like you are the right fit, please reach out to us with your profile and portfolio.
