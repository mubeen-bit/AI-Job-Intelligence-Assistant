## Description
Job Title: Senior Backend Developer (RoR)
We are an outsourcing company working for clients based around the world. We are looking for a Ruby on Rails developer responsible for the development and maintenance of a cross-platform application at an innovative startup.
Your primary focus will be the development of the core backend engine and its integration with multiple frontends across mobile and web. You will be working alongside other engineers and developers working on different layers of the infrastructure. You will be part of a startup environment with a small, agile team that moves fast and uses cutting-edge technologies, tools, and processes. Therefore, a commitment to collaborative problem solving, sophisticated design, and the creation of quality products are essential.
Responsibilities :
Development, delivery, and maintenance of platform functionality and features.
Development, delivery and maintenance of APIs exposing platform functionality.
Design and build applications using the Ruby on Rails framework and related technologies.
Improving code quality and maintainability by staying up-to-date on new technologies and development approaches.
Ensure the performance, quality, and responsiveness of the application.
Ensure that the platform is safe, secure, and compatible with the latest devices and OS versions.
Provide support for the development of multiple projects and devise solutions that are maintainable and scalable.
Identify, diagnose, and resolve production issues.
Identify opportunities for process and tool improvements, and perform the implementation.
Maintain transparent communication with team members and collaborate towards overcoming challenging hurdles.
Brainstorm, contribute new ideas, conceive innovative strategies, Identify and correct bottlenecks, and fix bugs.
Always be learning and working towards mastering your craft
Skills Required :
Minimum 5 years of experience in object-oriented backend development.
Minimum 3 years of experience building Ruby on Rails applications from scratch
Minimum 2 years of experience developing and maintaining APIs
Strong understanding of database design and Object Relational Mapping concepts
Experience building and extending data models in a low-complexity way using all types of ActiveRecord associations
Understanding of common Rails design patterns
Knowledge of the Rails ecosystem and the libraries available for common tasks
Solid understanding of agile application development lifecycle
Experience in web service and API integration (REST, JSON, and similar)
Excellent understanding of code versioning tools, such as Git.
Experience with network communications on mobile platforms, handling synchronous and asynchronous web service calls.
Must have developed Rails apps connected with mobile frontends
Experience with automated test suites using frameworks such as RSpec, Capybara, Cucumber, etc.
Experience with data migrations between different database technologies and schemas
Familiarity with cloud message APIs and push notifications.
Familiarity with building location aware apps, geofencing, Google Maps API integrations.
Familiarity with audio streaming
Familiarity with audio encoding and conversion of audio formats.
