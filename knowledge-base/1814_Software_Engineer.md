Excellent communication and collaboration skills. Ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a team.
Eye for detail - never letting things fall through cracks
Preferred Skills

Past experience managing sizable Hadoop cluster, with all included services
Full stack development experience
Past experience in Test Driven Development
