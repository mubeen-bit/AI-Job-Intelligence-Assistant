# Job Title: Senior Artificial Intelligence Engineer

## Job ID: 7

## Description

We Make Change is seeking a Senior Artificial Intelligence Engineer / AI Research Lead to help design, deploy, and optimise advanced AI systems focused on real-time and empathetic mental health support solutions.

The role involves building production-grade Large Language Model (LLM) and Agentic AI systems, integrating retrieval pipelines, and developing scalable backend services to support intelligent AI-driven applications.

## Responsibilities

- Design and maintain scalable FastAPI backend services for AI-powered applications.
- Develop and deploy Large Language Model (LLM) and Agentic AI systems.
- Integrate AI systems with Hugging Face-hosted models and LoRA adapters.
- Work with vector databases such as Milvus or Chroma for retrieval systems.
- Implement embedding models and custom prompt engineering pipelines.
- Fine-tune and optimise LLMs using Hugging Face PEFT and LoRA techniques.
- Build secure, reliable, and production-ready AI infrastructure.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to improve AI capabilities and user experience.
- Research and evaluate emerging AI technologies and methodologies.

## Required Skills

- Strong experience with Python and backend frameworks such as FastAPI.
- Hands-on experience with Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI systems.
- Familiarity with Hugging Face ecosystem and transformer models.
- Experience with vector databases and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems.
- Understanding of embeddings, prompt engineering, and AI optimisation techniques.
- Knowledge of machine learning workflows and model deployment.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Ability to work independently in a research-driven environment.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with Agentic AI systems and conversational AI.
- Familiarity with cloud infrastructure and scalable deployment pipelines.
- Understanding of AI ethics, privacy, and secure AI system development.
- Previous research or leadership experience in AI engineering.

## What the Role Offers

- Opportunity to work on impactful AI solutions for mental health support.
- Exposure to cutting-edge LLM, RAG, and Agentic AI technologies.
- Collaborative and mission-driven work environment.
- Opportunity to lead innovative AI research and engineering initiatives.
