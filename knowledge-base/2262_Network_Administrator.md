Job Description
As a senior Infrastructure Engineer you will be working in a challenging environment, supporting the business in day to day operations, as well as facilitating business changes.
The primary responsibility of this position is to develop, maintain, continuously improve and support both internal and client-facing IT infrastructure, including servers, storage, networks and telecoms systems.
You will be providing expert infrastructure oversight, ensuring successful delivery and performance of IT services for our clients and the fulfilment of our obligations and Service Levels Targets.
This role will be a major stakeholder in the successful growth of our service offering and expansion towards global markets.
Infrastructure
Research and identify the most appropriate options for the delivery infrastructure.
Design the architecture of the required infrastructure solution
Manage the deployment, maintenance and upgrade paths of the infrastructure
Identify, deploy and maintain appropriate connectivity options
Maintain and test Business Continuity plans and systems
Contribute to the overall quality of service delivered to internal users and clients.
Support the Application Support team in dealing with critical incidents in the Managed Service environment
Design
Contribute to the roadmap for the development and evolution of the infrastructure that is appropriate for the current needs as well as scalable for the future needs of the business
Continues improvement
Contribute toward design improvements that allow for more efficient support of the environment, applications and clients
Actively contribute to increasing the availability of our services by providing iterative improvements to existing infrastructure solutions

Proven ability to collaboratively pursue issue resolution and push for long term and permanent solutions to on-going problems
Understanding of fundamental computer architecture, networking, virtualisation, storage and operating system concepts
