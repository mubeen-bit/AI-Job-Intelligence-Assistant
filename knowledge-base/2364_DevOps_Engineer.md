# Job Title: DevOps Engineer

## Job ID: 2364

## Description
Who we Are:


DevX is the central tooling organization in the Core Software Group (CSG), responsible for the various SW development and test tools; customized for Cisco's engineering requirements and used in the developer's workflow. The core objective of the group is to improve engineering efficiency and provide better user experience by modernizing the tools used by Cisco's dev and test community

Who you Are:


You are a passionate engineer having excellent grasp of OS concepts & compilers with a hands-on experience working in an Build tools environment. You are passionate about developing quality software. You are eager & keep yourself updated on the standard methodologies, tools & technologies in the industry. You enjoy working in a team environment & demonstrate a strong can-do attitude.

Skills:
Shown experience in Build tools like Make or GMake
Hands on experience on sophisticated debugging and problem-solving skills
Expertise in either Python or C++ with Unix/Linux
Expertise in CI tools like Jenkins with prior experience in setting up CI/CD pipelines
Strong understanding of the compilation aspects with ability to work independently
Experience in enterprise embedded systems
Excellent verbal & written communication skills
Your responsibilities include:

Design and develop new software product features.
Give innovative ideas for multiple module architecture.
Define product internal/external interfaces.
Develop sophisticated, multiple modules of code independently.
Write complete functional and design specs independently.
Talk to other functional groups, evaluate and recommend tools
What You’ll Do:


You will be a part of a central team developing in-house solution for capturing real-time code coverage metrics on an embedded system. You will develop and sustain the solution across multiple product lines, various compiler flavors and OS’s; continuously exploring opportunity to further improve the solution with new functionality, approaches, or incorporating the latest developments.
