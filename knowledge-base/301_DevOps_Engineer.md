# Job Title: DevOps Engineer

## Job ID: 301

## Description
Be a part of our success. Sage Intacct is looking someone who has a passion for technology and a knack for communicating with developers.

As our engineering team grows, so does our infrastructure. We are growing rapidly and need to provide automation to solve these challenges. As a DevOps engineer, you will be the first line of defense for the Engineering Organization. In this role you will assist with the tooling for test-automation, build automation, environment automation, in both our on premise and cloud environments. Your audience will include diverse engineering teams who work together to build innovative cloud-based financial solutions.

Responsibilities:

Help shape the foundation of a newly formed DevOps team
Help define the tooling necessary to automate builds, testing, and cloud environments
Collaborate with multiple teams and domain experts to build/maintain CI pipelines
Work closely with Development and QA teams to understand the build, release, branching and versioning requirements for code and SQL Migrations
Assist with Cloud related projects using tools like Packer, Terraform, Ansible, github, etc.
Drive automation and reliability at all levels
Maintain a conscious level of security during all aspects of work performed

Requirements:

3-5 years of Engineering or IT related experience
Understanding of developer tasks and processes
Extensive experience building CI/CD pipelines
Experience in automation via shell scripting and using Configuration Management Tools
Ability to read and understand one or more programming languages
Knowledge of standard internet protocols
Experience with AWS services
Experience with tooling and systems for build, infrastructure automation, monitoring, etc.
BE/BTech/MCA in Computer Science or related areas
