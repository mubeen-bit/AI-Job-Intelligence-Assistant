# Job Title: Software Engineer

## Job ID: 2285

## Description
STP Investment Services is seeking a self-starter and motivated Senior Software Engineer specializing in Eagle Suite of products to join them in this newly added role. The Senior Software Engineer specializing in Eagle will perform a wide range of tasks including development of new applications, testing, supporting legacy client systems, configuring and interfacing with various capital market systems, and troubleshooting and improving current operational systems at both STP and client sites. This position is a very diverse position that presents a multitude of learning and growth opportunities for the successful candidate. This person will also need strong technical skills to lead development efforts and perform regular mentoring and training for other team members.
The successful candidate will possess a Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, or Master of Computer Applications (MCA). Preferred candidates will have at least 3 years of professional experience working in the Capital Markets industry with various web, database, and system technologies, especially Eagle Suite of Products. Candidates must be able to work with minimal supervision within a structured environment, possess excellent teamwork, organization, troubleshooting and communication skills. Additionally, the successful candidate must be enthusiastic, diligent, and take initiative and pride in their work. Self-motivation and the ability to learn quickly and deliver are essential skills and the key to success in our dynamic environment.
