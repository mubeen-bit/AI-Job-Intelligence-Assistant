# Job Title: Network Administrator

## Job ID: 546

## Description
Job Description:
Overview:
Agnik is a global connected car technology company (www.agnik.com) operating in 200+ countries headquartered in the USA with major consumer brands like Vyncs (www.vyncs.com) and several B2B products. Agnik is looking for a computer Security & Systems Administrator to join its Team at its New Town, Kolkata office. If you want to join a top-notch connected car applications development team and have at least three years of hands-on experience in systems administration, then apply with the resume and with the subject line “Computer Security & Systems Administrator”.
Responsibilities:
As an employee of Agnik, the primary focus of the Computer Security & Systems Administrator will be to maintain, secure, and improve the company computing environments. The primary focus is on the server, cloud, and desktop administration and support.
Systems Administrator duties include:
· Installation, maintenance, and upgrades on computer hardware and software systems including operating systems, web services, and databases.
· Monitoring Linux and Windows servers
· Monitoring performance of cloud-based distributed computing system and optimizing system behavior.
· Computer threat management and system security protocol design and enforcement using firewalls, intrusion detection systems, malware control, and other related technologies.
· Taking system backup in a data-intensive cloud-based data management system.
· Managing user access and passwords.
· Proposing and implementing systems enhancements that improve the reliability and performance of the system.
· Monitoring usage and performance of the network.
· Training personnel on system usage.
· Troubleshooting server, software, and hardware issues.
· Assisting with recommending, scheduling, and implementing system hardware and software upgrades or repairs.
· Researching, evaluating, and recommending software and hardware products.
· Providing new hardware specifications to users based on application needs and anticipated growth.
· Performing other related duties as assigned.
Qualifications
The Systems Administrator position requires:
· At least a B.Tech/BS degree from a reputed University in Computer Science (or other engineering/Science major) with relevant experience.
· 2 or more years of experience in systems administration.
· Basic working knowledge of Active Directory and Group Policy
· Windows Server 2012 knowledge
· Windows and Linux System Admin background
· Network Monitoring background
· Should provide identification/fixing for the problems within existing systems design/implementation of new systems
· Excellent problem-solving skills
· Knowledge in Systems and multi-tier application and Network Troubleshooting
· Strong Troubleshooting & Communication skills
· Excellent written & oral communication skills
· Ability to work both independently and strong collaboration within teams.

New Town, Kolkata.
