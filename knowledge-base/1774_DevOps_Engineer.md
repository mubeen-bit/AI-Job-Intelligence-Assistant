# Job Title: DevOps Engineer

## Job ID: 1774

## Description
We are searching for a "Systems Engineer - Collaboration services" to join our Corporate IT Operational Team.

The Systems Engineers primary focus is day-to-day operations of Lionbridge servers, networks, and computer systems in Lionbridge's datacenters and global data networks.

You are focused on monitoring of business-critical systems to prevent delays and outages in our high-availability datacenter and networks and finding and resolving irregularities and performance issues. You help coordinate and execute change projects to IT backend system and environments working with other Corporate IT teams.

Experience: 5+ years

What you will do:
Coordinate and implement changes to IT backend system and environments working with other Corporate IT teams.
Perform research and analysis of reported problems and issues, develop and recommend solution
Maintains configuration management tools and processes to support configuration identification, control, reporting, and delivery of IT services/systems.
Involvement in operational planning, creating standard operating procedures and policies.
Must be highly organized and capable of performing multi-tasking in a fast-paced environment and take part in 24\7 work rotations.
To be successful you will have:
Bachelor or Master in IT of Science in Information Systems or its equivalent
Strong verbal and written English communication skills
Broad infrastructure IT experience and competence and specialist knowledge in one of more relevant areas of collaboration services, such as managing configuration of Microsoft technologies such as Sharepoint Online, Skype, Teams, Email services, conference services, ect
Self-motivated with a demonstrated ability to work independently and as part of a team
Knowledge of and experience with automation and scripting tools such as SQL, Python, Powershell ect
Experience with working in a global team across multiple time zones
