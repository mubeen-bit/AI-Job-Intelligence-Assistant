# Job Title: Wordpress Developer

## Job ID: 978

## Description
Employment type: Full-time

If you are an enthusiastic person wanting to learn and explore the various possibilities and magic you can create using WordPress, come to join our team of WordPress Developers.

Brainstorm Force is currently building WordPress themes & plugins that are available publicly for downloading as products. As a WordPress Developer, you will be working with a knowledgeable and dedicated team known for world-class products like Astra, the Ultimate Addons for Visual Composer, the iMedica theme, ConvertPlus and more.

Roles and responsibilities

You will be working with an extremely passionate & smart team of WordPress geeks.

Have to contribute to building WordPress products (themes & plugins) that will be used by thousands of people across the world.
Expected to maintain, upgrade & support our existing portfolio of WordPress products that have crossed 100,000 downloads collectively.
Have to talk with end WordPress users & developer directly to understand their need in a better manner.
You will get an experience of working with international reputed clients from various industries.
Skills required

WordPress is built on PHP with HTML, CSS & JavaScript. So besides these obvious skill sets, WordPress developer should have a good understanding of it’s CMS functionalities, codebase architecture & most importantly – community & entire ecosystem.
