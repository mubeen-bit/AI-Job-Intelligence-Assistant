Performance tester/engineer with proven experience in performance testing of web-based/client-server applications and backend testing

Serve as a coordinator for all SPLI testing activities on assigned projects
Uses functional knowledge of applications to provide technical assistance in identifying, evaluating, and resolving moderately complex SPLI test problems
Handle multiple projects simultaneously
Prioritize tasks to ensure high quality services are being provided to clients
Author Performance test plans, and serve as a coordinator for all testing activities
Perform SPLI tests to ensure all deliverables are production ready and as per SLAs
Provide technical guidance of performance testing to application developers, architects, business partners and management
Train new team members on Performance testing & Engineering and methodology
Troubleshoot and resolve Performance Engineering issues
Have deep knowledge on Heap Memory and Garbage collection concepts
Perform performance Engineering tasks concurrently on multiple projects
Experience and Exposure to DevOps tools like Maven, GitHub, Udeploy, Artifactory, Sonar and Jenkins
Exposure and experience in Agile development life cycle
May have to work in off hour shift starting from 1:30 PM to 10:30 PM

Bachelors in Engineering or equivalent with minimum 6-8 years of experience with Performance testing /engineering using LoadRunner
Very strong in scripting using VuGen is desired
Basic knowledge on DevOps, Continuous Integration and Continuous Development activities
Good to have Cloud based performance Testing experience
Good to have certification on Load Runner or other performance Testing/Engineering tools and Application performance Monitoring tools
