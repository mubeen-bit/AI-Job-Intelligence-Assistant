# Job Title: Junior Data Analyst

## Job ID: 3

## Description

A company is seeking a motivated and detail-oriented Junior Data Analyst to join its growing analytics team. The role focuses on ensuring data integrity, transforming raw information into actionable insights, and supporting strategic decision-making across the business.

---

## Responsibilities

- Collect, clean, and validate data from multiple sources.
- Analyse datasets to identify trends, patterns, and business opportunities.
- Create dashboards, reports, and visualisations for stakeholders.
- Support senior analysts and management with data-driven recommendations.
- Maintain data accuracy, consistency, and quality standards.
- Assist with database management and reporting processes.
- Work collaboratively with different departments to improve operational efficiency.

---

## Required Skills

- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
- Good knowledge of Microsoft Excel and data reporting tools.
- Familiarity with SQL and database querying.
- Understanding of data visualisation tools such as Power BI or Tableau.
- Attention to detail and accuracy in handling data.
- Strong communication and teamwork skills.
- Ability to present insights clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

---

## Preferred Qualifications

- Degree in Data Analytics, Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, or a related field.
- Knowledge of Python or R is considered an advantage.
- Previous internship or project experience in analytics is beneficial.

---

## What the Role Offers

- Opportunity to work in a fast-growing data-driven environment.
- Professional development and mentorship from experienced analysts.
- Hands-on exposure to business intelligence and reporting tools.
- Career progression opportunities within analytics and business intelligence.
