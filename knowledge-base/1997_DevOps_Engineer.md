Position Overview :

Autodesk is looking for Site Reliability Engineer to join our talented Operations Engineering and Architecture team. If you are a reliability engineer or DevOps engineer, we want you to join!Autodesk, the leading Design and Engineering software company, is transitioning to the cloud! As a global leader in 3D design, engineering, and entertainment software, Autodesk helps people imagine, design, and create a better world. Autodesk accelerates better design through an unparalleled depth of experience and a broad portfolio of software to give customers the power to solve their design, business, and environmental challenges. In addition to designers, architects, engineers, and media and entertainment professionals, Autodesk helps students, educators, and casual creators unlock their creative ideas through user-friendly applications.

What drives you? Are you passionate about AI, Automation, Innovation and Cloud Ops technologies? Come work with the team and use your expertise and skills to design, build and support the platforms. You will be working closely with the engineering and enterprise application teams to ensure systems are secure, highly available and scalable. Your experience and scripting skills will help you automate scaling and management of server resources and implement code for zero-downtime deployments and to facilitate DevOps practices. This position offers an outstanding opportunity for a unique individual who possesses solid scripting background

Responsibilities :
Responsible for defining systems architecture of Cloud Hosted enterprise solutions
Implement Continuous Delivery mechanism
Automate the manual processes using suitable scripting language
Clearly document architecture & infrastructure
Define & adhere to rigorous change management policies
Develop appropriate technical diagramming to show interconnectivity of interacting systems and networks.
Drive and improve performance, scalability, security and availability
Monitor, recommend and implement tools
Optimize scaling/performance of networks, servers, monitoring and storage systems.
Implement preventative and corrective measures
Liaise with hosting partners to troubleshoot problems.
Perform root cause analysis of critical outages
Responsible for driving the process for efficient (and complete/secure) backup and restore
Minimum Qualifications :
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or a related field, plus 7 years of progressive professional experience
2-4 years’ experience in Java programming
AWS 2+ years of experience with knowledge on AWS technologies like EC2, Lambda, ELB, S3, CloudWatch, IAM, KMS, VPC, Dynamo DB, RDS.
DevOps experience, with automation experience with configuration tools and management tools.
Scripting skills - Python and shell scripting.
Problem Solving Ability – Ability to analyze and resolve infrastructure issues and deployment issues.
Develop scalable Cloud solutions
Experience with creation and maintenance of Hosting Environments
Experience with Maven, Jenkins and GitHub
Experience with Apache, Tomcat, Jetty or similar servers.
Understanding, diagnostics, troubleshooting of Java based applications
Experience with automation or scripting (e.g. Shell/Python/Perl/Ruby)
Experience with Linux and Windows operating systems
Knowledge of networking and security concepts
Strong written and verbal skills
