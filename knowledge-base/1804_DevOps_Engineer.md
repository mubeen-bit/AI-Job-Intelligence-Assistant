JOB DESCRIPTION:

Exciting opportunity to join the DevOps Centre Of Excellence as a DevOps Engineer.

The successful candidate will work with teams across the business. Working with multiple technology stacks in a wide variety of environments. The role will involve:

o Creating and enhancing automation (CI/CD)

o Expanding the toolchain functionality and availability, including adding integration points to the toolchain API

o Working with teams to improve processes

o Defining and delivering key development and operational patterns and practices.

The candidate will need to have a positive attitude and a strong desire to learn. The team works very closely together and the candidate will need to be a strong fit able to work with all team members as well as independently.

Key to success in the role is:

o Able self manage multiple concurrent small projects, including managing priorities between projects

o Strong problem solving

o Able to quickly learn and on-board new technologies

o Strong communication and documentation skills

The successful candidate will work with the following key technologies and should be skilled in at least 2 of the following, with knowledge of at least 4

o Jenkins Build Pipelines (Groovy)

o Node.js

o AWS CloudFormation

o PowerShell

o Bash

o Docker

o AWS Services (EC2, ECS, S3, IAM, SNS, SQS, Lambda, API Gateway)
