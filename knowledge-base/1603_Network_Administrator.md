Roles and Responsibilities
Monitoring of all infrastructure, development and environmental alerts to ensure 24x7 operations and minimal service impacts
Provide early warning and rapid response for each alert
Perform first level troubleshooting
Validates the alert and escalate to appropriate IT technicians to have the issue investigated further
Update and maintain the NOCs document library
Ensures all activities are documented and tracked through the NOC ticketing system
Works with both internal and external teams to assist in escalated troubleshooting for issues relating to the company's products and platforms
Executes established procedures as it relates to incident, change and event management
Handle trouble tickets and work closely with engineering and field technicians
Open and Escalate trouble tickets with carriers as well as with hardware vendors to resolve service impacting issues
Escalate and communicate alerts within the prescribed SLA
Recommend solutions to correct issues and faults
Submit Change Control Tasks for carrier maintenance
Participates and/or be part of the NOC continuous improvement activities as defined
Perform other duties as assigned by management

Desired Candidate Profile
Advanced understanding and experience in VPN technologies and network protocols like BGP, OSPF, EIGRP
CCNP Routing and Switching Certification is mandatory
CCIE and/or with additional certification and experience on Network will be preferred
Education
Bachelor's degree in Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, Information Technology or any related course
Candidate should have minimum of 5 years network operations or engineering experience
Candidate should have advanced experience in handling a wide array of Cisco platforms like routers, switches, firewalls and WLAN controllers
Candidates having experience in Talari or any SD-WAN platforms will be preferred
