# Job Title: DevOps Engineer

## Job ID: 791

## Description
Vista Equity Partners is the world's leading private equity firm for Enterprise Software and recently invested $1.5B in the Reliance Jio Digital Platform. As part of the Vista portfolio, Integral Ad Science (IAS) is a NYC-headquartered global technology and data company that builds verification, optimization, and analytics solutions for the advertising industry. We are looking for a Senior Systems Engineer to join our new and fast-growing R&D team in Pune. If you are excited by technology that has the power to handle hundreds of thousands of transactions per second; collect tens of billions of events each day; and evaluate thousands of data-points in real-time all while responding in just a few milliseconds, then IAS is the place for you!

As a Senior Systems Engineer, you will help automate and grow our infrastructure which is a global mixture of physical and cloud systems. The ideal candidate possesses an advanced level of knowledge of Linux systems and a firm grasp of all of the layers involved in a complex, high throughput, and low latency environment. The candidate will put that knowledge to use in troubleshooting, optimizing, and automating as much as possible.

What you'll get to do:
Support global production systems by participating in shared on-call rotations
Capacity planning and growing the infrastructure to handle increasing traffic at a global scale
Research and development of new technologies
Work with developers to optimize applications and infrastructure
Serve as a subject matter expert and train junior staff
Who you are and what you have:
5+ years Linux server administration experience (software, hardware, network troubleshooting)
Coding ability in Bash and Perl, Python, Ruby or similar
Automating administration tasks, configuration management (Puppet or similar), and monitoring
Experience utilizing Amazon Web Services
Network and systems security and optimization
BS in Computer Science/related degree or equivalent experience
What puts you over the top:
Relational (MySQL/RDS) and columnar (Snowflake) database administration
Familiarity with administration and use of Elastic.co, Graphite, Zabbix, Kafka, Big Data tools
Ability to debug Java/Tomcat applications
About Integral Ad Science

Integral Ad Science (IAS) is the global market leader in digital ad verification, offering technologies that drive high-quality advertising media. IAS equips advertisers and publishers with both the insight and technology to protect their advertising investments from fraud and unsafe environments as well as to capture consumer attention, and drive business outcomes. Founded in 2009, IAS is headquartered in New York with global operations in 18 offices across 13 countries. IAS is part of the Vista Equity Partners portfolio of software companies. For more on how IAS is powering great impressions for top publishers and advertisers around the world, visit integralads.com.
