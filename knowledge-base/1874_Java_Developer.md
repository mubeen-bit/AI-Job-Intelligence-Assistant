The Position: * We're looking for an energetic, self-motivated software developer to join our development team. Responsibilities include developing web-based, desktop, cloud, and API applications using C#, Visual Basic .NET, and the .NET Framework. The applicant will meet regularly with company management and senior developers to discuss project specifications and their implementation. In turn, the applicant will develop components to that specification.
The developer is responsible for full lifecycle development including requirement analysis, coding, unit testing, and documentation. The developer should have a strong working knowledge of the internet and good working experience with SQL and relational databases.

The developer works to understand business requirements and objectives and collaborates with the senior architect to outline technical solutions. The developer may work with a team or independently within a project domain and is responsible for delivering all functionality within an application module. In other words, a full-stack developer is required.
Requirements: *
Comfortable acting as a full-stack developer

Ability to analyze and troubleshoot existing program code to isolate and correct issues
Ability to work as part of a team with specific direction, or as an individual project lead with only high-level direction

Attention to detail

Ability to meet deadlines and hold oneself accountable
Above all, we are looking for exceptionally smart, professionally motivated, and driven people who can join our team and contribute for years to come. Apply if you feel you are ready to elevate your experience through exposure to multiple projects across various applications.
