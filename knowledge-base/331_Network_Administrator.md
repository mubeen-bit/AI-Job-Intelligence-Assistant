# Job Title: Network Administrator

## Job ID: 331

## Description
Responsibilities
· Perform LAN/ WAN debugging
· Perform routing configuration on all networking equipment
· Perform Fortinet Firewall installations/ configurations
· Perform wireless access points installations and administration
· Manage performance and maintain security of LANs at an intermediate level
· Assist in implementation of policies and procedures for LAN/ WAN usage throughout the organization
· Upgrade and maintain routing equipment across our LAN/ WAN environment
· Monitor all communication equipment in support of new product / process implementations
· Documents all changes, upgrades, maintenance, procedures, etc. using the new document infrastructure
· Manage LAN/ WAN traffic analysis and monthly performance reports of all network segments
· Recommend use of new products and services to senior management
· Responsible for all remote user and router account terminations (passwords, etc.) when needed
· Diagnose and repair problems, investigate system errors, and ensure user accessibility
· Required Skills
o Strong oral and written communication skills, as well as the ability to explain highly technical concepts to a non- technical audience
o Troubleshooting and problem resolution skills
o Able to function independently and in a team environment with minimal supervision
· Qualifications
o 5+ years experience with routers and switches, implementation and support of complex LAN/ WAN environments
o 2+ years experience with firewall administration- preferably Fortinet and with Load Balancers
o Bachelor's Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or Engineering, or equivalent work experience
· Familiar with network security principles, tools, and best practices
· Solid understanding of routing and TCP/ IP, BGP, OSPF, VPN, MPLS, ,
· Solid understanding of switching protocols, VTP, Spanning Tree, Port Security, QoS
· Experience with network traffic analysis and protocol analyzers
· Manage desktop environments for the organisation
· Camera
· Security alarm device
