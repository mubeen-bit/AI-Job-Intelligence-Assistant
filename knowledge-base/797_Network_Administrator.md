Your Role and Responsibilities
As a Network Administrator, you are responsible to maintain a reliable, secure and efficient data communications network. You will configure, maintain and monitor all active network equipment in order to ensure smooth network operation.

You are self-motivated and willing to learn about new technologies, vendors, and IBM / client environments. Solving problem and network skills are required. You will be a team player with proven communication skills, and that is willing to work with other teammates to aid / support to both our internal and external clients.
Responsibilities
Responsible for recovering provisioning failures and will be first point of contact for any provisioning related issues
Configuration, implementation & troubleshooting in the field of Cisco IP telephony Network Infrastructure
Demonstrate expertise in switching domain such as VLAN, Voice VLAN, VTP, STP, PoE, Ether channel, HSRP, Routing Protocols, etc.,
Review each provisioning request / incident and plan the activities with minimal impact to customer environment
Manage Cisco Unified Communication Products such as Cisco Unified Communications Manager (CUCM) – 9.x, 10.x, 11.x



Required Technical and Professional Expertise
Minimum 5+ years of experience in IT domain
Proficient in Cisco Unity Connection, Cisco Unified Presence.
Solid understanding of Cisco Unified Communication Express & Cisco Unity express Gateways such as Cisco Analog Gateways, H.323 Gateway, MGCP Gateway, SIP Gateway, CUBE.
Validated knowledge of Trunks such as SIP Trunks, E1/ T1(CAS,CCS), FXS /FXO etc
Hands-on experience on Cisco Unified Contact Center Express, Prime License Manager, UCS.
Solid understanding of Gateway protocols such as H323, SIP, MGCP, SCCP.
Validated knowledge on Endpoints like Cisco IP Phones, Cisco IP Communicator, Jabber, SIP Soft Phones

Preferred Technical and Professional Expertise
You should be able to work in collaborative environments that use agile methodologies to encourage creative design thinking and find innovative ways to develop with cutting edge technologies
Ambitious individual who can work under their own direction towards agreed targets/goals and with creative approach to work
Intuitive individual with an ability to manage change and proven time management
Proven interpersonal skills while contributing to team effort by accomplishing related results as needed
Up-to-date technical knowledge by attending educational workshops, reviewing publications
