# Job Title: Network Administrator

## Job ID: 1315

## Description
Experience with databases, networks (LAN, WAN), printers and patch management. He should be responsible for Installing and configuring software, hardware and networks, software Antivirus installation and configuration, Mail configuration on mobile devices.
He should be capable to Diagnose, troubleshoot and resolve hardware, software or other network and system problems, and replace defective components when necessary.
He should be responsible to maintain the timely back up of the important data and management resource, taking care of daily, weekly, monthly, yearly backups of all servers and send backups reports to concern IT head on daily basis.
Should have good knowledge of system security (e.g. intrusion detection systems) and data backup/Restore
He should be responsible to ensure security through access controls, backups and firewalls.
Should be able to diagnose & resolve problems quickly.
Should have very good interaction with each staff member and provide help desk support for system related issues, Network issues (LAN & Wi fi), Printer and other peripheral devices issue, for internal users and remote users.
Responsible to maintain the detailed and latest inventory records and asset management.
