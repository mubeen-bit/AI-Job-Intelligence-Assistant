Job Requirements:

Basic Qualifications:
Detailed knowledge of WordPress, PHP and MySQL (this is not an entry-level position)
Strong JavaScript, HTML and CSS skills
Experienced in troubleshooting and supporting sophisticated WordPress sites
Knowledge of hosting environments, domains and DNS
Comfortable with working in a fast-paced, technical support environment
Have a positive demeanor and are comfortable working independently and as a member of a hardworking team
Have outstanding written and verbal communication skills
Passion for the web, open source development, and overall client success
Strong problem-solving abilities, initiative, and are comfortable performing under pressure
Good knowledge of WordPress security features, plug-ins and know-how to secure WordPress Enterprise sites.
Extra credit:

Skillset of orchestration technologies such as Kubernetes
Knowledge of configuration management tools such as Ansible
Experience with containerization technologies such as Docker
Knowledge of or experience with other CMS technologies and/or open-source technologies
Crafting and implementing microservices
Comfortable with large scale task automation
Cloud hosting experience (e.g., Amazon Web Services, Google Compute, Azure)
