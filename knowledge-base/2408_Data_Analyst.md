# Job Title: Data Analyst

## Job ID: 1

## Description

Step into a role where your data skills shape decisions that impact millions. We’re looking for a Data Analyst who’s ready to dive into new tools, smarter technologies, and fresh ways of working as we tackle today’s challenges and prepare for tomorrow. From SQL to Power BI to Python, you’ll turn complex data into clear insight that drives real change across Water Networks Operations.

## What You’ll Be Doing as a Data Analyst

- Working with large data sets drawn from a wide variety of internal and external sources, including environmental data, survey feedback and operational data.
- Design and interrogation of databases; carrying out data transformations and liaising with the business to improve our ways of working.
- Supporting the business and working with stakeholders to design, build, and maintain a range of reports.
- Becoming a key point of contact, collaborating with colleagues, and engaging with stakeholders to further our objectives.
- Informing colleagues of operational decisions.
- Maintaining data quality and ensuring data is secure and properly utilised in line with GDPR.
- Bridging the gap between business and data using analytical thinking to solve business problems and generate value through clear presentations and insight.

## Location

Hybrid – Kemble Court – RG2 6AD

## Working Pattern

- 36 Hours Monday – Friday
- 1 day a week in the office every Tuesday

## What You Should Bring to the Role

- A positive, curious mindset and a natural interest in solving problems with data.
- Experience working with large datasets, databases or reporting.
- Strong understanding of operational data and confidence to learn quickly.
- Experience with query languages like TSQL or ANSI SQL.
- Experience with scripting languages such as Python or R is an advantage.
- Experience with dashboarding software like Power BI or Tableau.
- Familiarity with tools like Business Objects, Microsoft Azure services, or Microsoft Office.
- Ability to explain complex information clearly to different audiences.
- Degree (or equivalent experience) in an analytical subject such as Science, Maths or Geography is an advantage.
- Experience applying analytical skills in a business setting and supporting stakeholders.

## Benefits

- Competitive salary up to £45,000 per annum depending on experience.
- 26 days holiday increasing to 30 with service.
- Performance-related pay plan.
- Generous pension scheme through AON.
- Health and wellbeing support including physiotherapy and counselling.
- Cycle to Work scheme, shopping vouchers and life assurance.
