Essential skills to be considered for the role:

· 8+ Years of experience in to Network engineering having strong technical skills.

· Previous experience in a similar role with a Managed Services Provider or large Enterprise Business, supporting UK and EU based customers

· Familiarity with content switching / network load balancing technologies such as F5 or Netscalers.

· Excellent spoken English

· Comprehensive knowledge of network infrastructure, including the ability to analyse and debug layers 1-7.

· Experience of troubleshooting enterprise LAN’s

· Experience of troubleshooting enterprise WAN’s

· Managing Telco’s throughout Incidents

· Experience with Cisco layer 2 and 3 switches.

· Experience of firewall and VPN and troubleshooting Cisco ASA firewalls.
