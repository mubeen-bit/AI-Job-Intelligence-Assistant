Job Description:

Who are we?

As a company built on the foundation of Privacy by Design, for more than 20 years Neustar’s unique capabilities have made us the leader in the field of responsible identity resolution. Neustar enables trusted connections between companies and people at the moments that matter the most, with world-class and industry leading solutions in Marketing, Risk, Communications, Security and Registry services.

We are looking for a test engineer for our product portfolio of Trusted Call Solutions Group. Neustar is a pioneer in Emerging Secure Telephony STIR/SHAKEN Technology and building up world class infrastructure on Micro Service Model to support Tier1/Tier2 customers in this area. Not only we support whole stack of STIR/SHAKEN solution, we also offer supplementary Services on top of this infrastructure supported by Neustar’s Caller ID platform with 90% of the US Market. We are looking for a test engineer in this domain who has working experience with SIP Telephony and RESTful services along with solid automation experience.

Job Duties

1. Setup the infrastructure in cloud and local networks for End to End solution of Secure telephony. (10%)

2. Identify various test cases/scenarios based on Product Requirements for REST and SIP interfaces. Ensure proper QA policies and testing coverage is applied to assigned projects and tasks (20%)

3. Interactions with various teams for requirement Analysis, Design discussions, Implementation and testing. Write user stories and tasks for sprints, coordinate efforts and communication with the developers and other team members to ensure resolution of defects, and manage/triage defects in the bug tracking system(10%)

4. Automate the test cases using industry standard tools for both SIP and REST interfaces. Drive automation initiatives including white box scripting and functional automation projects (20%)

5. Execute Test cases for any new release. Consult with various cross department team members to review and troubleshoot production issues (20%)

6. Identify Scenarios for various custom implementation and verify those scenarios. Ensure that products meet or exceed the quality standards by applying knowledge of quality assurance standards and procedures

(10%)

7. Setup Jenkins, CI/CD Orchestration based on Docker images for various components (10%)

8. Prepare detailed test reports

Qualifications/Education
Bachelor’s (B.S) or Master’s (M.S) degree in Computer Science or related field
6+ years recent experience in Software Development and/or Software Test Automation
The candidate should be well versed with SIP Telephony concepts. Any experience on Secure telephony (STIR/SHAKEN) is a huge plus.
The candidate should have working experience of testing RESTful services through various standard tools.
Must be proficient in a Linux/Unix environments
The candidate should have good test automation experience using industry standard tools on REST API and SIP Telephony.
The candidate should be well versed with Devops Orchestrations models e.g. Ansible, Docker, CI/CD, Jenkins etc.
Any experience working on cloud infrastructure is a plus.
Understanding of the entire Quality Assurance process, including defect submission standards, test case and test plan documentation
Strong technical and analytical skills related to software Quality Assurance
Excellent verbal and written communication skills
Experience as a Scrum Team member
Exposed to Test Driven Development (TDD)
Self-motivated and able to thrive in a results-driven environment
High energy, well-organized, team player
