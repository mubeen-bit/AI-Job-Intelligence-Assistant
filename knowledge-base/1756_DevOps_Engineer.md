# Job Title: DevOps Engineer

## Job ID: 1756

## Description
At Fortanix, we are redefining cloud security. We are seeking DevOps engineers to build, maintain, and improve our production infrastructure and development tools. The DevOps Team has wide-ranging impact at Fortanix: we ensure that all Fortanix Engineers can reliably, efficiently, and safely deliver our product to customers; we measure, monitor, and automate whenever and whatever we can; we continually look for ways to improve ourselves and our tools.

You will:
Contribute to a friendly and helpful company culture
Build and maintain automation to improve the efficiency of all Fortanix engineers
Define the production metrics we should track, and ensure we’re monitoring them.
Participate in on-call rotation
Contribute to defining security best-practices, and ensuring our infrastructure adheres to them
Write and deploy code, and continue to find ways to make it easier for your peers to do the same.

Requirements

You are someone who:
Exhibits uncompromising integrity
Pays attention to detail
Thinks critically about complex technical problems
Can work independently, while also being an effective member of a team
Is interested in continual learning and improvement
You have:
7+ years of software engineering experience, preferably in a DevOps-related role
Proficiency in a scripting language, such as Python, Ruby, Perl, or Bash
An understanding of networking protocols, such as IP, DNS, TCP, TLS
Experience with Linux administration
Experience two or more CSPs (Azure, AWS, GCP, Alibaba Cloud, IBM cloud, Oracle cloud etc)
Experience with two or mode suits of VMware (vSphere, vCenter, vSAN), vCloud Director, vRealize, Tanzu etc
Orchestration frameworks like Kubernetes, OpenShift
Configuration and provisioning management tools like Chef, Ansible, and Puppet
Monitoring tools such as Sensu, Prometheus, Nagios or similar* Continuous integration systems like Jenkins and Travis

Benefits

Free catered lunch every day
Friendly culture that brings the best out of everybody
