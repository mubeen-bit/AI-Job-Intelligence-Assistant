Why work at DCKAP?

At DCKAP you will have the opportunity to be part of a highly active ecosystem which is transforming the way the world looks at eCommerce. You will be responsible for both back-end and front-end development, including creating WordPress themes and plugins.

Primary Job Responsibilities:

Hands-on software development skills, deep technical expertise across the entire software delivery process.
You will work closely with the Marketing team to write well documented code, design, implement and manage the WordPress websites.

Job Requirements:

Excellent knowledge of WordPress, PHP, MySQL, CSS, HTML and jQuery.
Experience creating custom WordPress CMS websites and plugins using the best practice.
Theme/template integration and creation of custom post types.
Experience working on any one of the page builders is an added advantage.
Strong understanding of latest industry trends and content management systems.
Experience using SVN software and GIT.
Good organisational skills with the ability to track and prioritise numerous tasks.
Proactive personality with a proven track record of following through and delivering on responsibilities.
Highly adaptive and capable of being an individual contributor and working as part of a team.
