# Job Title: DevOps Engineer

## Job ID: 743

## Description
Job Description:


Job Title DevOps Lead

Location India

Corporate Title Assistant Vice President

Overview

As DevOps Lead in RFT, you will be working as a part of DevOps transformation team that work to implement DevOps strategy and drive the DevOps transformation across RFT domain. In this role, you will work in partnership with the Agile, Testing and Release Management functions to proactively engage test teams to make sure that deliveries are aligned with overall transformation objectives. You will be required to help the subdomains identify, assess, manage the DevOps health needs including any bespoke solution to improve the KPIs.

What we’ll offer you

A healthy, engaged and well-supported workforce are better equipped to do their best work and, more importantly, enjoy their lives inside and outside the workplace. That’s why we are committed to providing an environment with your development and wellbeing at its center.

You can expect:
Competitive salary and non-contributory pension
30 days’ holiday plus bank holidays, with the option to purchase additional days
Life Assurance and Private Healthcare for you and your family
The opportunity to support a wide ranging CSR (Corporate service responsibility) programme.
Your key responsibilities
Working with other engineers to support our adoption of continuous delivery, automating the building, packaging, testing and deployment of applications
Creating the tools required to deploy and manage applications effectively in Test Environments, with minimal manual effort
Helping teams to adopt modern delivery practices, such as extensive use of automated testing, continuous integration and more frequent releases.
Configuring and managing code repositories, continuous builds, artefact repositories, cloud platforms and other tools
Contributing towards a culture of learning and continuous improvement within your team and beyond
Sharing skills and knowledge in a wide range of topics relating to Devops and software delivery
Your skills and experience
Experience creating and maintaining automated builds, using tools such as Team City, Jenkins, etc.
Hands on experience of scripting languages, such as bash, ruby or python. Some experience with general-purpose programming languages – preferably Java
Knowledge and experience on configuration management tools such as Ansible, puppet.
Good understanding of version control systems, branching, merging, etc. Practical experience using Git
Using repositories such as Nexus and Artifactory to manage and distribute binary artifacts
An understanding of automated testing, using tools such as Junit, Cucumber, Selenium etc.
An understanding of operating systems, platforms and infrastructure. Including Unix and Windows, Docker, PaaS and IaaS technologies such as Open Shift.
Ability to manage multiple stakeholders and work with both technical and non- technical individuals on a daily basis testing and development lifecycles
