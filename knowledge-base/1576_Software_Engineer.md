# Job Title: Software Engineer

## Description
Who we are: Fueled by a fundamental belief that having access to financial services creates opportunity, PayPal (NASDAQ: PYPL) is committed to democratizing financial services and empowering people and businesses to join and thrive in the global economy. Our open digital payments platform gives PayPal’s 286 million active account holders the confidence to connect and transact in new and powerful ways, whether they are online, on a mobile device, in an app, or in person. Through a combination of technological innovation and strategic partnerships, PayPal creates better ways to manage and move money, and offers choice and flexibility when sending payments, paying or getting paid. Available in more than 200 markets around the world, the PayPal platform, including Braintree, Venmo and Xoom enables consumers and merchants to receive money in more than 100 currencies, withdraw funds in 56 currencies and hold balances in their PayPal accounts in 25 currencies.

Job Description Summary: We are looking for a full stack engineer with experience in building high-performing, scalable, enterprise-grade applications. You'll be part of a cross-functional team that's responsible for the full software development life cycle, from conception to deployment and further. As a full stack developer, you should be passionate about bringing great experiences to life and comfortable working with front-end and back-end tech stacks, development frameworks and third-party libraries.

Job Description:

Responsibilities include,

Design and develop highly scalable and performant applications for mission-critical systems
Writing clean, functional code as well as test automation on the front-end and back-end
Review/refactor, design and code to continuously improve the deliverable quality
Work with product managers, architect development teams to deliver high quality software solutions
Design, develop and maintain client-side and server-side software components including web applications, webservices/REST API, real-time systems and batches
Build features and applications with a mobile first design
Prototype ideas quickly using cutting edge technologies
Provide technical solutions to meet business requirements
Actively mentor and coach engineers on technology and engineering aspects
Collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams

Requirements:

Bachelors/Masters in computer science with 8 to 10 years of experience in Software Development
Experience in full life cycle of software development including software analysis, design, development, testing and maintenance
Experience on common stacks across front-end and back-end tech stacks
Experience on multiple front-end languages and libraries (HTML/CSS, JavaScript, React)
Experience on multiple back-end technologies (node.js, Java, Spring)
Experience working on webservices/REST API, event driven real-time systems and batch components
Experience with mobile application and chatbot development is good to have.
Knowledge with SQL/NoSQL databases (e.g. Oracle, MySQL, MongoDB), web servers (e.g. Apache) and UI/UX design
Proven expertise in software craftmanship, code quality and refactoring
Expertise in secure coding practices and software engineering best practices
Strong skill in data structure and algorithm
Knowledge with cross-browser, cross-platform, accessibility testing
Experience with Continuous Integration and Delivery
Experience with unit testing/functional automation testing and tools
Self-motivated individual with ability to adapt to fast-paced environment
Strong Problem-solving, analytical ability
Excellent communication and soft skills
