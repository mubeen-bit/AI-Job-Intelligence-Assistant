# Job Title: Principal AI Engineer

## Job ID: 8

## Description

WeBuild-AI is seeking a Principal AI Engineer to lead the design, development, and deployment of advanced Artificial Intelligence systems and machine learning solutions. The role focuses on building scalable AI infrastructure, driving technical innovation, and leading engineering efforts across cutting-edge AI initiatives.

The ideal candidate will combine strong software engineering expertise with deep knowledge of machine learning, Generative AI, and production-scale AI systems.

## Responsibilities

- Lead the architecture and development of scalable AI and machine learning systems.
- Design and deploy production-grade AI solutions and intelligent automation pipelines.
- Work with Large Language Models (LLMs), Generative AI, and advanced machine learning frameworks.
- Collaborate with product, engineering, and leadership teams to define AI strategy and technical direction.
- Build reliable APIs, backend services, and cloud-native AI infrastructure.
- Optimise AI model performance, scalability, and deployment workflows.
- Mentor engineers and contribute to technical leadership across AI initiatives.
- Research and evaluate emerging AI technologies and best practices.
- Ensure AI systems are secure, efficient, and production-ready.

## Required Skills

- Strong expertise in Python and modern software engineering practices.
- Extensive experience with machine learning and AI frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, or Hugging Face.
- Experience working with Large Language Models (LLMs) and Generative AI systems.
- Strong understanding of distributed systems, cloud platforms, and scalable architectures.
- Familiarity with MLOps, CI/CD pipelines, Docker, and Kubernetes.
- Experience designing APIs and backend systems for AI applications.
- Excellent analytical, leadership, and problem-solving abilities.
- Strong communication and stakeholder collaboration skills.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience leading AI engineering teams or enterprise AI projects.
- Familiarity with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems and vector databases.
- Knowledge of cloud AI services on AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
- Experience building AI-powered products in production environments.

## What the Role Offers

- Opportunity to lead innovative AI and Generative AI initiatives.
- Exposure to cutting-edge machine learning technologies and enterprise-scale AI systems.
- Collaborative and fast-paced engineering environment.
- Leadership and career growth opportunities within AI engineering.
