About the team -Climate 21

We are the SAP S/4HANA Sustainability team and are part of the Product Engineering organization at SAP. We work on innovative cloud applications. Our applications are productively used by customers, powered by state-of-the-art technologies, and provide cloud qualities such as elastic scaling and high availability.

Addressing climate change and sustainability has moved to the top of priorities for many companies. A new sustainability solution by SAP will enable our customers to address this global and urgent issue. The solution shall help them assess, analyze, and act on the CO2 footprint of their products and operations, on their journey towards a sustainable, low-carbon economy.

As part of the sustainability project the SAP S/4HANA Sustainability team develops cloud native applications and services to support our customers in understanding and acting on their sustainability impact in all relevant business processes. The sustainability framework will be seamlessly integrated with all major SAP offerings like SAP S/4HANA, SAP Ariba, and others.

The Role
Climate 21 is a new project currently in concept and technical design phase. The overall project team will be based out of multiple locations in India and Germany. The teams in India will have full ownership of certain key topics.
For this team in Bangalore, we are looking for passionate (Associate) Developers, with the following expectations and tasks:
You contribute to the success of our customers by working in a team that implements a completely new sustainability framework based on latest Cloud native technologies.
You participate in technical design discussions and feel empowered to bring your expertise into the discussions.
You are eager to make a lasting impact by developing Cloud applications that help customers embed sustainability into daily business processes.
You work together with a team in an agile process. We expect you to collaborate strongly as well as to be able to work individually.
You are excited to learn about new technologies and evaluate how they can help in the context of our applications.
Our customers rely us to deliver products they can use for business-critical processes. You always keep quality in mind and automated testing and DevOps principles come naturally to you.
