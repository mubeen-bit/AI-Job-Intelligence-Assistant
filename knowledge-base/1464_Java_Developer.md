Who we are

Texas A&M University’s Enterprise Information Systems (EIS) department provides reliable and cost-effective, mission-critical, enterprise-wide information to the Texas A&M University’s main campus (including School of Law, Health Science Center, and other locations across Texas) and its branch campuses in Galveston and Qatar to support the academic, administrative, research and recruiting missions of Texas A&M University. EIS systems allow students to apply, enroll, pay and graduate, then transition from Texas A&M University to careers. The EIS team manages the information in the Compass system, a software product called Banner, which assists academic services to ensure effective and efficient processes, accurate and timely reporting, and quality services for students, faculty and staff.
