Duties & Responsibilities:
Design and implement new features and functionality.
Creating and supervise the website’s architecture.
Ensuring high-performance and availability and manage all the technical aspects of the CMS.
Formulate effective, responsive design and turning it into a working theme and plugin.
Creating WordPress themes and plugins.
Leading website performance tests.
Troubleshooting content issues.

Monitoring the performance of the live website

Strong Technical Knowledge of front-end technologies, including HTML5, CSS3, Javascript Jquery.

Have clarity and understand CSS changes and their effects while ensuring consistent style across all platforms and browsers.
Experience to convert comprehensive layout and wireframes into working HTML pages.
Knowledge of how to interact with RESTful APIs and formats (JSON, XML).
A strong understanding of code versioning tools, including Git, SVN, and Mercurial.
Strong knowledge of PHP back-end development is required.
Qualifications:
Graduate/post-graduate in the technical field of BCA, Bsc- IT, BE, B.Tech, MBA-IT, MCA, Msc-IT.
Experience of minimum 2 – 5 yrs.
