# Job Title: Business Analyst – Automation, Analytics & AI

## Job ID: 4

## Description

The company is seeking a Business Analyst to support Automation, Analytics, and Artificial Intelligence initiatives. The role focuses on identifying business opportunities, improving operational efficiency, and enabling data-driven decision-making through automation and advanced analytics solutions.

The Business Analyst will work closely with business stakeholders, technical teams, and cross-functional departments to gather requirements, analyse processes, and support implementation of AI and analytics-driven solutions.

---

## Responsibilities

- Collaborate with stakeholders to understand business needs and translate them into technical requirements.
- Analyse business processes and identify opportunities for automation and optimisation.
- Support development and implementation of analytics and AI-driven solutions.
- Create process documentation, workflows, and business requirement documents.
- Assist in project planning, testing, validation, and deployment activities.
- Work with data teams to interpret analytical findings and generate actionable insights.
- Monitor project performance and help ensure successful delivery of automation initiatives.
- Communicate findings and recommendations clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.

---

## Required Skills

- Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities.
- Experience in business analysis, process improvement, or analytics-focused roles.
- Understanding of automation technologies, analytics platforms, or AI concepts.
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
- Ability to work collaboratively across teams and departments.
- Experience with reporting, dashboards, or business intelligence tools is beneficial.
- Familiarity with Agile methodologies or project management practices is an advantage.

---

## Preferred Qualifications

- Bachelor’s degree in Business, Data Analytics, Information Systems, Computer Science, or a related discipline.
- Experience with SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Python, or automation platforms is considered valuable.
- Interest in emerging technologies including AI and machine learning.

---

## What the Role Offers

- Opportunity to work on innovative AI and automation initiatives.
- Exposure to enterprise analytics and digital transformation projects.
- Collaborative and technology-driven work environment.
- Career growth and professional development opportunities.
