# Job Title: AI Engineer

## Job ID: 6

## Description

Work2gether is seeking an AI Engineer to design, develop, and deploy intelligent systems powered by Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning technologies. The role involves building scalable AI-driven solutions, integrating machine learning models into production environments, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to solve complex business problems.

The ideal candidate should have strong programming skills, hands-on experience with machine learning frameworks, and an interest in developing innovative AI applications.

## Responsibilities

- Design and implement AI and machine learning solutions for business applications.
- Develop, train, evaluate, and optimise machine learning models.
- Work with large datasets to extract insights and improve model performance.
- Collaborate with software engineers, product teams, and stakeholders to deliver AI-powered systems.
- Build and maintain scalable AI pipelines and deployment workflows.
- Research and evaluate emerging AI technologies and methodologies.
- Improve automation, predictive analytics, and intelligent decision-making systems.
- Ensure models are reliable, efficient, and production-ready.

## Required Skills

- Strong proficiency in Python and machine learning development.
- Experience with AI/ML frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Scikit-learn.
- Understanding of deep learning, NLP, or computer vision concepts.
- Knowledge of data processing and model deployment techniques.
- Familiarity with cloud platforms and scalable architectures.
- Experience with APIs, databases, and software engineering best practices.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or a related field.
- Experience working with Generative AI, LLMs, or automation tools is advantageous.
- Familiarity with MLOps, Docker, Kubernetes, or cloud AI services is beneficial.

## What the Role Offers

- Opportunity to work on cutting-edge AI technologies and projects.
- Exposure to innovative machine learning and automation initiatives.
- Collaborative and fast-paced engineering environment.
- Professional development and career growth opportunities.
