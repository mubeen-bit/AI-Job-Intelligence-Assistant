# Job Title: Software Engineer

## Job ID: 2032

## Description
Infoblox is seeking a Software Engineer I to join our team in Bangalore. You will be part of our highly successful product portfolio for Telecom Service Providers and ISPs. You will report to the Manager, Software Engineering based in Bangalore.

In this role, you will have an opportunity to work on NFVs, cloud technologies, customer requirements, and feature development of existing products. You will develop firmware as well as drivers for the Infoblox DNS platforms.

Responsibilities:
Develop software for DDI, security products, and NFV implementations
Execute fast-path packet processing development at the embedded level
Carry out firmware and driver development for the DNS and security platforms
Develop hardware abstraction layers and APIs
Execute kernel and platform related implementation
Ensure performance optimizations and bug fixes
Design, document, implement, and unit-test firmware and drivers
Work with QA and manufacturing team to help ensure a high-quality product
Support the sustaining engineering team for any customer escalations
Requirements:
3+ years of hands-on experience in software development
Minimum of 2 years experience in embedded firmware development
Strong C/C++ programming skills in Linux or UNIX multi-core environment
Strong networking background and hands-on experience in networking software development
In-depth, hands-on network packet processing knowledge and experience with Ethernet, IP, UDP, TCP protocols
Experience with designing, documenting, and implementing software features in embedded networking products
Experience with processors such as ARM, Cavium, and PowerPC
Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and willingness to figure out solutions
Work completely and accurately under time constraints and deadlines
Education:
A four-year degree in CS, CE, EE, or equivalent is required
Master’s in CS, CE, or EE is preferred
