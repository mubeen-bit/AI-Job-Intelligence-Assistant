# Job Title: DevOps Engineer

## Job ID: 993

## Description
Roles and Responsibilities

Programming experience with different languages such as Python//Java in a Linux development environment
A broad range of technology interests across different disciplines including computer and system architecture, web applications, performance analysis, distributed systems design, testing and verification technologies, data processing, and networking
Ability to work on distributed remote agile teams
You would be working side-by-side with some of the best people in the industry on a Google Cloud Platform (GCP) team creating automation to provision and configure infrastructure (Cloud instances, containers, networks) and automate the delivery of software to the Cloud using CI/CD tools and processes

Required experiences and skills

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science Engineering or a related field
2+ years of experience with software development in building distributed applications and systems or equivalent expertise
Experience with scripting languages such as Bash, PowerShell, Ruby, or Python
Preferably knowledge with DevOps Orchestration/Configuration Management technologies such as Chef, Puppet, Docker, Jenkins, Ansible, etc
Knowledge in cloud technologies such as AWS/GCP

What do we look for in our team members?

Ambitious and Resourceful
Passionate and Positive
Independent Contributor
Good Listening Skills
Organized and Process Oriented
Out of the Box Thinker
Personable and Adaptable
