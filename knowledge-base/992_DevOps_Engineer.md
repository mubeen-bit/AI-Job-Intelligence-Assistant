# Job Title: DevOps Engineer

## Job ID: 992

## Description
EdCast is the AI-Powered Knowledge Cloud solution for unified discovery, personalized learning, and knowledge management across the enterprise. Its award-winning platform is used internationally by Global 2000 companies and large government organizations, including NASSCOM and World Economic Forum, to solve the discovery and curation problems across all external and internal knowledge sources. EdCast's offerings include its Learning Experience Platform (LXP) and MyGuide's in-app guides, intelligence, and automation for business software.

Website: - www.edcast.com

Job Title: DevOps Engineer

Location: Mumbai, India.

Year of experience: 2-4 years

Requirements

Key Result Areas include:

Lead and participate in designing and implementing the cutting edge DevOps solutions for EdCast's global operations.
Build and manage production, QA and development environments in AWS and GCP cloud.
Work on assigned tasks to deliver on DevOps’s team’s key objectives of system availability, performance and cost management.
Work with security team on Implementing security processes and controls
Work closely with our developers to troubleshoot application and infrastructure issues
Participation in the selection and implementation of new tools and technologies to support new product initiatives
Be part of DevOps’s 24/7 on-call support and troubleshoot production issues as required
Manage assigned tasks in JIRA and communicate issues and status to stakeholders

You Are:

A driven team player, collaborator, and relationship builder whose infectious can-do attitude inspires others and encourages great performance in a fast-moving environment.
Results orientated, motivated by success.
Self-motivated: You can work with a minimum of supervision and be capable of strategically prioritising multiple tasks in a proactive manner.

What you need:

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or closely related subject.
2-4 years of system administration and DevOps experience in AWS and GCP environments
2-4 years of experience in Kubernetes preferably in AWS environment.
Experience with DevOps CI/CD and monitoring tools like Jenkins and NewRelic
Programming and debugging experience in one of the most popular languages (Python, Node.JS or Ruby).
Good understanding of system availability, security and performance management.
Experience with Git or similar tool
Excellent troubleshooting skills and desire to resolve system issues
AWS and GCP certifications a plus

Benefits

An opportunity to get ahead in your career by building a great and exciting portfolio that helps others get ahead in theirs. You will be joining a truly passionate and multicultural team willing to make a difference and positively disrupt the way learning is done within organizations.

We believe in lifelong learning. We believe we can create abundant opportunities for workforce learning and up-skilling within the organization ecosystems. We have the spark, the ambition, the drive and the passion of making an impact in the world.
