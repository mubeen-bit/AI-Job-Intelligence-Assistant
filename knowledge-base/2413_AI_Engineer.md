# Job Title: Artificial Intelligence Engineer

## Job ID: 9

## Description

A company is seeking an Artificial Intelligence Engineer to help design, develop, and deploy scalable AI-powered systems and machine learning solutions. The role focuses on building production-ready AI applications, improving intelligent automation processes, and supporting advanced analytics initiatives.

The ideal candidate will have strong software engineering and machine learning experience, with the ability to work across the full AI development lifecycle from research and prototyping to deployment and optimisation.

## Responsibilities

- Design and implement AI and machine learning models for real-world applications.
- Develop and maintain scalable AI pipelines and backend systems.
- Work with structured and unstructured datasets to generate actionable insights.
- Build and optimise Large Language Model (LLM) and Generative AI solutions.
- Collaborate with engineering, product, and business teams to deliver AI-driven products.
- Evaluate and integrate emerging AI technologies and frameworks.
- Improve automation, prediction systems, and intelligent decision-making workflows.
- Monitor, test, and optimise AI model performance in production environments.
- Ensure scalability, reliability, and security of AI systems.

## Required Skills

- Strong proficiency in Python and AI development workflows.
- Experience with machine learning frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, or Scikit-learn.
- Knowledge of Generative AI, LLMs, NLP, or deep learning techniques.
- Familiarity with APIs, cloud platforms, and scalable architectures.
- Experience with SQL, databases, and data engineering concepts.
- Understanding of MLOps, CI/CD pipelines, Docker, or Kubernetes.
- Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills.
- Excellent communication and collaboration abilities.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Degree in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field.
- Experience with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems and vector databases.
- Familiarity with AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud AI services.
- Experience deploying production-grade AI systems is advantageous.

## What the Role Offers

- Opportunity to work on cutting-edge AI and machine learning projects.
- Exposure to enterprise-scale AI engineering and automation initiatives.
- Collaborative and innovation-focused work environment.
- Professional growth opportunities within AI and advanced analytics.
