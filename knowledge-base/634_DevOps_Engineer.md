# Job Title: DevOps Engineer

## Job ID: 634

## Description
POSITION PURPOSE: Provide support, automation, and infrastructure expertise for all aspects of the Johnson Controls IoT SaaS offerings. Expertise will be centered on Cloud (particularly, but not limited to Azure) environments, with specific emphasis on Linux, Kubernetes, security, ISO, SOC2 and CI/CD pipeline automation.

What you will do

The Senior DevOps Engineer is responsible for the automation and configuration management of the platforms infrastructure and provides stable environments for delivery of quality. They will work as part of a team continuously delivering and deploying to customers.

The Senior DevOps Engineer is responsible for the automation of all manual tasks for the building and deployment of both code and data in order to enable continuous integration and continuous deployment frameworks.

The Senior DevOps Engineer is also tasked with the creation of software deployment strategies that allow team members to successfully deploy sites/software in any environment. They are also responsible for the updating of configuration management tools, code, and modules for the purpose of streamlining implementation and supportability.

The Senior DevOps Engineer is further tasked with implementing monitoring and logging solutions that identify both system bottlenecks and production issues. They will further identify and implement data storage schemas such as clustering in order to maximize performance.

How you will do it

A candidate for this position must have had at least 5+ years of working experience managing infrastructure, 2+ of this as a DevOps Engineer. The candidate must also have had experience automating the managing of large-scale infrastructure and have significant familiarity with Chef, Ansible, or similar configuration management tools. The candidate will have had vast experience in systems automation, orchestration, deployment, and implementation.

He will have had excellent in-depth experience using Docker, Kubernetes/Openshift, Jenkins and Linux as well as experience configuring and automating monitoring tools. The candidate must similarly demonstrate experience in software development and experience working with tools and languages such as .Net Core , Angular, Java ,Git, Python, Shell scripting, Java, and SQL.

A suitable candidate will further demonstrate experience in scaling distributed data systems and
2+ years experience in building, maintaining and operating production systems (> 99.5% SLA) on Microsoft Azure(Preferred) ,GCP or AWS
